I'm a developer who moved into security. These days I work between code and
infrastructure: reading applications from the inside, testing them like an
attacker, and helping teams fix what turns up.

I like the practical side: finding the cause in the code, proving the impact on
the system, and leaving behind a fix the team can actually use. In practice,
that ranges from code review and AppSec to internal pentesting, Active
Directory, and red teaming across AWS, Azure, and GCP.

## What I do

- **AppSec and code review** — reading code with a developer's eye: OWASP Top 10, authentication and authorization, dependencies, secrets, SAST/DAST, and security in CI/CD.
- **Pentest and red team** — testing web applications and APIs, exploiting issues such as SQLi, XSS, SSRF, and RCE, and writing technical and executive reports.
- **Internal environments and Active Directory** — enumeration, ACL abuse, Kerberoasting, AS-REP Roasting, lateral movement, and paths to Domain Admin.
- **Cloud offensive security** — service enumeration, IAM/RBAC, tokens, compute, and storage across AWS, Azure, and GCP, with scenarios aligned to MITRE ATT&CK Cloud.
